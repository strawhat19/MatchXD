# MatchXD Design Examples

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the existing MatchXDv1.png brand reference. These are visual concepts, not implemented screens. All shown profiles are fictional adults.

- **01 — Coral Classic:** Bright white, coral actions, geometric typography, and familiar discovery cards. The recommended starting direction for everyday usability.
- **02 — First Chapter:** An editorial profile layout with serif headings, pale pink accents, generous spacing, and prominent bios.
- **03 — After Hours:** Charcoal surfaces, immersive photography, large actions, and a desktop connections panel. A candidate for the dark theme.

Each concept includes a mobile discovery view and a desktop app layout. Final implementation should use the supplied brand assets directly; generated typography and small UI details are illustrative.

## Prompt 01 — Coral Classic

Use case: ui-mockup.
Transform the supplied MatchXD brand-concept reference into a new high-fidelity UI design presentation titled "01 / CORAL CLASSIC". The reference is the branding source, not a layout to reproduce: preserve the MATCHXD heavy charcoal uppercase wordmark and the distinctive coral/pink crossed-two-people X symbol, also reuse the coral rounded-square app icon. Do not redesign the brand.
Create one exceptionally polished landscape board, 3:2 aspect ratio, high resolution, front-facing flat UI, no perspective. A generously sized mobile discovery screen at the left and a wider desktop dating-app workspace at the right, equal visual quality and readable UI. They should fill nearly all the board beneath a compact title, with plenty of orderly spacing and no overlap.
Art direction: crisp white and very pale cool-gray surfaces, charcoal #24262E, saturated coral #FF5267, soft pink #FFA5AD. Friendly, modern geometric sans typography resembling Poppins. 20px rounded photo cards, fine gray dividers, very subtle shadows, clean icons. Restraint and coherence, not a landing page. Tiny palette chips near the title.
Mobile: clean white header with the actual small MATCHXD wordmark and "33 XOs" wallet pill. Heading "Discover" and small location "New York" beside a filter button. One beautiful large portrait card of a fictional 27-year-old adult woman, Sofia, outdoors in natural daylight, fully clothed casual style, candid inviting expression, realistic rather than airbrushed. Bottom of photo has restrained legible white "Sofia, 27" and "3 miles away". Below, interest pills "Coffee" "Hiking" "Design". A row of distinct round rewind, pass, coral heart like, and super-like actions. Bottom navigation: Discover, Matches, MXO, Profile; Discover selected in coral. Comfortable native-app spacing and touch targets.
Desktop: white rounded browser/app viewport with thin top chrome. Narrow left sidebar with MATCHXD and icons/text Discover, Matches, Messages, MXO, Profiler, Profile. Discover selected with pale pink background. Main column header "Discover" and "New York · Within 25 miles", Filters button, large Sofia photo card and swipe controls. Right column is profile details with "Sofia, 27", location, short bio "Designer, weekend hiker, and always looking for a good coffee spot.", interest pills, two small secondary photos, and clear "Like Sofia" action. Header shows 33 XOs and a user avatar. No marketing hero, no ads, no fake metrics.
All profile imagery is fictional adults. Minimal board footer "MATCHXD / DESIGN CONCEPT / DEMO PROFILES". Exceptional typography hierarchy, no tiny illegible paragraphs, no purple, no gold, no beige, no 3D decorations, no overflowing elements. The result should look feasible to implement in Expo, with a polished premium dating-app interface.

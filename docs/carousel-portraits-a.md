# Carousel Portraits — Set A

Generated September 10, 2026 for MatchXD's illustrative landing carousel.

## Provenance

Six new fictional adult portraits were generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool (`image_gen__imagegen` in the tool bridge), using one call per asset in two concurrent groups of three. No CLI/API fallback was used. No reference images were passed to the generation calls. Existing project portraits were viewed only to understand the natural lifestyle style; no existing face was used as an edit target or identity reference.

The tool returned 1086 × 1448 PNGs, exactly 3:4, rather than the ideal 768 × 1024 requested in the prompts. The original pixels were retained: no resizing, cropping, retouching, or format conversion was performed. Generated originals remain at their source paths below, and byte-identical copies are saved under `assets/profiles/carousel/`.

These are AI-generated fictional characters, not real members, verified identities, or photographs of named real people. Names and ages are illustrative character details supplied in the generation brief.

## Review

Each of the six returned images was visually inspected for a distinct face and requested setting, complete head framing with space above the hair, natural texture and lighting, appropriate clothing, and absence of foreground text, logos, watermark, frames, or interface elements. Aisha uses a leafy bookshop courtyard, Nina a pottery studio, Isabel a market, Zoe a lakeside path, Priya a botanic garden, and Lucia a seaside cafe. PNG dimensions and matching SHA-256 hashes were checked after copying. No application source edits, tests, or builds were performed for this asset task.
